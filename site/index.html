Extending Li–Poon–Sze's elementary-symmetric realignment comparison to unequal dimensions

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 3 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordDOILean source

Abstract

Extending Li–Poon–Sze's equal-dimensional answer (2011, Theorem 2.4), we answer yes to their open problem (P2), arXiv:1103.2499v2, p. 4, for every 2 <= m < n and 2 <= l <= m^2, by combining a covariance bound with explicit positive block states. The maximal degree-l elementary symmetric function of realignment singular values over separable states is strictly smaller than its maximum over states satisfying the realignment criterion. The original question is Lupo–Aniello–Scardicchio (2008), Section 6, p. 15, item (ii). One matrix works simultaneously at every nontrivial degree, and small white-noise mixtures give full-rank examples strictly inside the realignment constraint.
